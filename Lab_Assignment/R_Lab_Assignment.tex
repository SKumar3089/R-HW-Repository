In this exercise, we analyzed the housing dataset containing information
on neighborhood, borough, market value per square foot, and year built.
After cleaning the data, descriptive statistics and graphical
visualizations demonstrated that location plays a more critical role in
determining property value than building age. While newer constructions
often had higher market values, older properties in desirable locations
remained competitively priced. The distribution of market values was
heavily right-skewed, with a concentration of moderately priced
properties and a smaller number of high-value outliers. Significant
variability within boroughs further highlighted the diverse nature of
local housing markets.
